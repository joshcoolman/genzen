A location reference sheet drawn from the reference images: one wide,
establishing view of the place itself, empty of people.

Show more of it than the references do -- they are glimpses caught behind the
action, and this is the whole space: its layout, its surfaces, its light, what
stands in it. Even daylight or the place's own practical lighting, nothing
theatrical. No text, no labels, no borders.

Keep the architecture, the materials and the objects from the references. Leave
out every figure, and do not invent a different place.
