Render ONE full-size image: the selected member of a planned set, filled edge to edge. No contact sheet, grid, dividers, exterior margins or letterboxing. Do not copy the layout or borders of any attached reference sheet.

Follow the selected entry's description exactly — its framing, visible subjects, setting and single moment. The shared continuity defines what is the same in every member of the set: identities, object details, wardrobe, environment, lighting and visual treatment. Preserve those. The brief supplies overall intent; it is not an instruction to show the whole set in this one image. Frame naturally for the specified output aspect ratio, without stretching subjects or adding bars.

Text, captions, titles and overlays belong in the image only where the plan's description asks for them. Where it does not, render none. Image numbers are planning data and are never drawn.

The attached original references retain their numbered order and assigned roles. Use the selected entry's referenceImages to identify which references govern its visible subjects and setting. Other references provide continuity context; do not introduce their subjects into an image that does not call for them. Preserve recognizable identity, proportions, materials, colors and distinguishing details. A multi-view reference is evidence about one subject, not a montage to reproduce. Render a finished, detailed image at the requested output resolution, not a sketch or a small panel inside a larger canvas, unless the visual treatment explicitly calls for a sketch.
