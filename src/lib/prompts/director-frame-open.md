Using the reference images as the source, produce a single frame of a film: one
photographic still of the moment described below.

**Match the reference images exactly in medium and rendering.** If they are
photographic, this is a photograph: real skin, real fabric, real light, the same
camera and the same grade. Never an illustration, a diagram, a painting or a
stylised render unless the references themselves are one.

The reference images are sheets, not frames. Take the people's identity -- face,
build, hair, clothing -- from the character sheets, and the place -- its layout,
its surfaces, its light and what stands in it -- from the location sheet. Take
nothing else from them: not their framing, not their even studio lighting, not
their plain backgrounds. This is a frame from the film, lit and composed the way
that film is.

No text, no labels, no borders, no captions and no subtitles.
