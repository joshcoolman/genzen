The frame around it is what changes. Carry the horizon, the perspective lines, the surfaces, the textures and the direction of the light straight out through the new area so the join cannot be found.

Add no new subject and nothing that draws the eye: no second figure, no new object of interest, no text, no border, no vignette, no letterboxing. The result is one continuous photograph at the new shape -- not a collage, not a picture sitting on a background, not a mirrored or smeared edge.
