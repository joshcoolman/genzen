Generate one continuous shot of a film. The first frame is supplied, and the
video begins on it: the shot continues from that exact composition, light and
framing rather than cutting to another one. The remaining reference images are
the character and location sheets this frame was drawn from, and they are there
so the identity and the place hold; they are records, not frames, and their
plain backgrounds and even studio lighting are not this shot's.

One shot. No cuts, no transitions, no montage, no title cards, no text on
screen.

**Sound: no music of any kind.** No score, no underscore, no swell, no theme, no
instruments, no song. Only what the place and the action make -- room tone, air,
distance, footsteps, cloth, the sounds of the space itself -- and the voice
below when there is one. Music is added later over the finished cut, and a
score generated separately for each shot would change key at every join.
