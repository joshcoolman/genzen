Two broad soft sources stand close in on either side of the subject, one cool and one warm, and they light the subject itself far more than anything else. The cool one washes right across the side of the face, the ear, the neck and the shoulder that turn away, tinting the skin there plainly and unmistakably. The warm one lies across the near cheek, the jaw, the chest and the clothing, tinting that skin just as plainly. The two meet along the centre line of the face and down the front of the throat and mix there without either winning, so almost no part of the subject keeps its original neutral colour. Behind them the same two colours continue onto the wall as two flat fields divided by one hard vertical edge that falls just behind the head. The light is soft and even, with no hard shadow edge anywhere, gentle falloff, the whole picture bright and low in contrast, the skin luminous rather than modelled. The cool source and its field are a desaturated sea green; the warm source and its field are a soft coral red.
