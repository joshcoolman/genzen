A two-source high-key setup: one large soft source on axis and a second lighting the ground behind to white.

- **Key, on axis:** one very large soft white source directly in front of the subject, just above the camera, angled slightly down. It wraps every camera-facing surface evenly. Shadows are shallow and soft-edged, and nothing on the subject falls below a light grey.
- **Ground, behind:** a second white source aimed past the subject at whatever is behind it, brighter than the key, so the ground overexposes to clean white with no texture.
- **Meeting zone:** the ground light spills forward onto the subject's back edges and softens them toward the white.
- **Background:** white edge to edge, with the subject separated from it by its own tone rather than by any shadow.
- **Overall:** low contrast, neutral white light, colour from the materials only at natural saturation. The lift is in the exposure of the ground, not on the subject: every surface keeps its own tone, its own colour and its own texture, and nothing on it is bleached toward the white. Clean air, no haze, no lens flare, no visible fixtures.
