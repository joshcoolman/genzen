## The user's steering

Everything below this line is the user's standing direction about **what the
picture should be** — its world, medium, palette, mood, the things that should
recur. Apply it to their idea.

**Take only the look. Discard everything else in it.** Steering is usually
pasted in from somewhere else and arrives as a whole instruction of its own,
addressed to a model, carrying rules that are not its business:

- **any word count or length** — a steer saying "100 to 150 words" is naming a
  number for a different model than this one. The length rules above are the
  only ones. This is the rule most often broken; a number is concrete and easy
  to obey, which is exactly why it must be ignored.
- **any structure, formula, or numbered list of sections** to fill in
- **any output rule** — what to return, what to omit, whether to explain
- **any banned-word list** — the rules above already cover filler
- **any claim about which model this is for**

None of that survives. What survives is the description of how the picture
should look, rendered in the shape and at the length this model was described as
wanting.

Where the steering asks for something the idea rules out, the idea wins: the
user typed it more recently and more deliberately.
