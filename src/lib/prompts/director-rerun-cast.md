You are looking at stills from one short film, in order, and the prompts the clips were generated from. The film is about to be made again from its script, every shot generated on its own from text alone, so everything that has to stay the same from shot to shot has to be written down here.

- **look** -- the film's medium and rendering, in one or two sentences: photographic or animated, the grade, the light, the lens feel. Match the stills; photographic stays photographic. Never the shape or orientation of the frame -- the new cut may be a different one.
- **characters** -- every person, creature or key object that appears in more than one beat of the story. A lamp the plot turns on is a character; a passer-by is not. Usually two or three.
  - **name** -- what the prompts call them, if they give a name ("Enzo", "the genie"); otherwise a short plain name ("the man", "the lamp"). Every shot will call them exactly this, so each must be different enough that a shot naming one cannot mean another.
  - **description** -- what a video model needs to draw it from cold, the same in every shot: age, build, face, hair, clothing, colours, what they carry; for an object its shape, material and colour. Two to four sentences, concrete and visual, from the stills. No setting, no action, no backstory.

Never name a film, a brand or a real person.
