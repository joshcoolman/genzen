A character reference sheet on a plain neutral background, drawn from the
reference images.

The same person throughout: front, side and back views of the full figure,
standing, plus a row of three or four head-and-shoulders expressions. Same face,
same build, same clothing, same proportions in every view. Even, flat lighting
with no dramatic shadow -- this is a sheet to work from, not a shot from the
film. No text, no labels, no borders, no grid lines.

The reference images are stills of this character. Keep the identity and the
clothing; ignore their framing, their lighting and whatever else is in them.
